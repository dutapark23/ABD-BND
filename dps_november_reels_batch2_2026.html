Ad D — Math Reel · Cold · "One Session = 30 Days of Content"
"Two hours.
Thirty days of
content. The math."
The content multiplication argument. Most people think about video production as a one-off cost for one piece of content. This ad reframes the math entirely — 2 hours in the studio produces enough content to post every single day for a month. When the numbers land, the objection of "it's not worth my time" disappears instantly.

Execution Notes
The stacked list is the visual — as each deliverable is said, show it appearing on screen as text. This is a simple CapCut or DaVinci edit: text appearing on beat. Takes 20 minutes to produce. The visual stack landing one by one is what makes this ad feel different from the others.
Adjust the numbers to DPS reality — "30 pieces of content" is the claim. Only use it if it's accurate. If a standard DPS session delivers 15 pieces, say 15. Specificity beats inflation every time.
Universal cold audience — this ad works without any prior knowledge of DPS. The hook is just math. Anyone who creates content — or wants to — will stop for it.

The self-identification hook — one of the highest-performing cold formats on Meta right now because the algorithm reads who stops and optimises toward that exact audience. No spray and pray. The viewer pre-qualifies themselves by staying. Andromeda then finds more people exactly like them.

Execution Notes
The algorithm learns from who stops — "Stop scrolling if…" is not just a hook device. Every person who pauses on this ad tells Meta exactly who your buyer is. After 200–300 stops, Andromeda has a precise model and starts finding more of them automatically. This is why the format works so well cold.
Three reasons they haven't started — "no team, no equipment, no time" covers the three universal blockers. If DPS research shows a fourth common one specific to Dubai, add it. But don't exceed four — the list needs to feel complete, not exhaustive.
Variant — swap "business owner" for the specific verticals DPS serves most: "Stop scrolling if you're a Dubai real estate agent / financial advisor / consultant who's been meaning to start video." One re-film of the first line, three targeted versions.
Ad F — Cost Reality Reel · Cold + Warm · "What a Video Team Actually Costs"
"Do you know
what a Dubai
video team costs?"
The price anchor. Most DPS prospects have no idea what professional video production costs in Dubai — so when they see DPS pricing, they don't know if it's cheap or expensive. This ad sets the anchor. Once they've heard the real market rate, DPS feels like a no-brainer. No discounts. Just context.

Execution Notes
Never say the DPS price in this ad — the entire mechanism is anchoring. If you say "and we only charge AED X", you turn this into a price comparison ad and lose control. The "WhatsApp us and ask — you'll be surprised" close is intentional: curiosity converts. Let the WhatsApp conversation do the price reveal.
Verify the Dubai market numbers — AED 3,000–5,000/day for a videographer and AED 8,000/month for an editor are real Dubai market rates as of late 2026. If DPS has more precise data from its network, use those exact numbers.
Works cold and warm — cold audiences are shocked by the market rate (creates instant value perception for DPS). Warm audiences who already know DPS are now re-anchored — "I didn't realise how much I'd save."

The sharpest urgency play of the campaign — and the most honest. December in Dubai means holidays, clients travelling, and decision-makers mentally checked out until January. This ad doesn't fabricate pressure. It explains why the window is actually November, not December. The most powerful close for warm leads sitting on the fence right now.
