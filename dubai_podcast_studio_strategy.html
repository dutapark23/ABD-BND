Section 01

Executive Summary

Dubai Podcast Studio is sitting on a genuinely rare asset: 7 professional studio spaces in one of the world's most visited and wealthiest cities, with verified social proof (3,500+ professionals, 75 countries) and confirmed celebrity-tier clients including Yomi Denzel and Oussama Amar — two of the most influential French-speaking entrepreneurs in the world.

The business is not a startup. It is a validated, operational facility with hard evidence of demand. The core problem is not product quality — it's that the marketing does not yet reflect the calibre of what exists inside those studios.

The Central Diagnosis

You are under-charging, under-communicating, and under-monetising an asset that is already world-class. The 90-day strategy below is designed to fix all three — without reinventing what works.

Three strategic priorities govern everything in this document:

  • Leverage what you already have — celebrity clients, 3,500+ client base, premium location. None of this is being used at full force yet.
  • Build recurring revenue — move beyond transactional hourly bookings toward monthly retainers, corporate programmes, and agency partnerships.
  • Own the international angle — "the podcast studio where the world comes to record in Dubai" is a positioning no local competitor can replicate. Own it aggressively.
Section 02

Market Diagnosis

What the Market Looks Like

Dubai is one of the fastest-growing podcast markets in the world. The UAE has among the highest smartphone penetration and English-language media consumption rates in the MENA region. Business Bay — where you operate — is home to tens of thousands of executives, entrepreneurs, and consultants who are precisely the demographic driving global podcast growth.

Simultaneously, Dubai has become a magnet for international content creators and digital entrepreneurs. The city attracts millions of high-net-worth visitors annually, many of whom are already podcasters or YouTubers looking to record while in Dubai.

What You Have That Others Don't

Asset 01

7 Studios in Business Bay

No other facility in Business Bay matches this capacity. You can run multiple simultaneous bookings — a revenue multiplier most competitors cannot access.

Asset 02

Netflix-Approved Equipment

Sony A7S III, Shure SM7B (same as Joe Rogan), Rodecaster Pro. This is broadcast-grade infrastructure that individual creators cannot replicate at home.

Asset 03

3,500+ Clients from 75 Countries

Proof of international demand. This statistic alone, properly deployed, makes you the obvious choice for any creator passing through Dubai.

Asset 04

Yomi Denzel + Oussama Amar

Two of the most influential French-speaking entrepreneurial voices in the world have recorded here. This is transformational social proof — currently massively underused.

The Critical Gaps

Gap 01 — Social Proof is Invisible: The testimonials section on the website currently contains placeholder content. You have verified real clients — Yomi Denzel, Oussama Amar, 3,500+ professionals — but none of this appears on the site with the weight it deserves. A single authentic testimonial from Yomi Denzel is worth more than any paid ad campaign.

Gap 03 — The B2B Market is Untouched: Business Bay is full of companies, PR agencies, and corporate communications teams who need podcast-format content. This is likely your highest-value untapped segment.
Gap 04 — SEO is Under-Optimised: Ranking for "podcast studio Dubai," "record podcast Dubai," and "podcast studio business bay" should be non-negotiable. These are high-intent searches with direct commercial value.
Gap 05 — Content Marketing is Absent: A podcast studio that doesn't produce visible, compelling content about its own product is leaving the most powerful proof of quality on the table.
Section 03

Strategic Positioning

Current Positioning

"Best podcast studio in Dubai" and "only studio available for rent in Business Bay." This is functional but generic. It describes a feature, not an identity. It gives a prospect a reason to consider you — but not a reason to choose you over a cheaper competitor or a home setup.

Recommended Positioning

Core Position Statement

"The studio where Dubai's most serious voices come to be heard — and where the world comes to record."

This positioning works on three levels:

  • Prestige + community: "Most serious voices" attracts ambitious, high-spending clients and repels bargain-hunters who will always be a cost centre.
  • International: "Where the world comes to record" activates the 75-country social proof and positions you as a destination, not just a rental space.
  • Authority: It makes every existing client feel that their choice was right — and it makes every prospective client feel that being there is an achievement.

The Brand Voice

Premium. Confident. Direct. Never aspirational in a vague sense — always grounded in specifics (the equipment, the names, the numbers). Think less "we help you podcast" and more "this is where Yomi Denzel records."

The Celebrity Anchor

Immediate Action Required

Yomi Denzel and Oussama Amar are not just clients. They are proof of category leadership for millions of French-speaking entrepreneurs, particularly across Africa, Europe, and the Arab world — a demographic with massive overlap with Dubai's international visitor base. Every piece of marketing material, the homepage hero, the booking page, and all social profiles should reference these names prominently. Not buried in a client list. Front and centre.

Section 04

Audience Segmentation

Not all customers are equal. These four segments are listed in order of strategic priority — based on revenue potential, lifetime value, and repeatability.

Segment 01 — Corporate & B2B Priority 1

Who: Companies, CEOs, PR agencies, consultants, and brands in Business Bay and DIFC who want to launch branded podcasts, internal comms content, or thought leadership content.

Why they matter: Highest lifetime value. A company that commits to a monthly podcast series could generate 20,000–40,000 AED per year from a single client. They book in advance, pay reliably, and refer other businesses.

  • Target via LinkedIn with direct outreach to CMOs, Communications Directors, and PR Managers in Dubai
  • Offer a dedicated "Corporate Podcast Programme" package with SLA, account manager, and branding options
  • Approach PR agencies as resellers — they bring multiple clients and earn a referral fee
  • Pitch to Dubai-based think tanks, universities, and government entities (EXPO legacy, Dubai Future Foundation, etc.)
Segment 02 — International Travelling Creators Priority 1

Who: YouTube creators, podcasters, and digital entrepreneurs who visit Dubai and want to record professional content during their trip. Particularly strong in the French-speaking creator ecosystem (North Africa, West Africa, France, Belgium) given the Yomi Denzel / Oussama Amar association.

Why they matter: High average booking value, strong tendency to share content from the studio (organic reach), and they validate the international positioning.

  • Create a dedicated "Record in Dubai" landing page targeting this audience explicitly
  • Partner with luxury hotels (Four Seasons, Address Hotels, Bulgari) to be featured in concierge guides and in-room directories
  • Target French-speaking creator Facebook groups and Discord communities with awareness content
  • Offer a "Creator Visit Package" — studio session + content kit (edited clips, thumbnails brief, social cutdowns)
Segment 03 — Dubai-Based Regular Podcasters Priority 2

Who: Coaches, consultants, entrepreneurs, and thought leaders based in Dubai who want to build a podcast as part of their personal brand strategy. They record regularly — weekly or bi-weekly.

Why they matter: Most predictable revenue. A recurring monthly client is worth far more than a one-off booker. This segment is the foundation of stable utilisation.

  • Create monthly retainer packages (e.g. 8 hours/month at a preferred rate) to convert one-time clients into recurring ones
  • Host a monthly "Podcasters Night" networking event in the studio to build community and referrals
  • Target LinkedIn and Instagram with content that speaks to coaches and consultants specifically
Segment 04 — Media & Content Agencies Priority 3

Who: Content production companies, media agencies, and video production houses that need a turn-key studio for client work without owning the infrastructure.

Why they matter: They book in bulk and bring predictable high-volume usage. A single agency relationship can be equivalent to 10 individual clients.

  • Offer agency-rate pricing with a formal partnership agreement
  • Provide white-label options (studio branding removed for agency-produced content)
  • Build a B2B sales deck specifically for agencies
Section 05

Revenue Architecture

The current model is purely transactional — hourly rates with no recurring component. The objective is to build a revenue structure that generates predictable monthly income while maintaining flexibility for walk-in and one-off bookings.

Proposed Product Ladder

Tier Package Name Price (AED) Target Segment Revenue Type
Entry Single Session (1–3h) 350–450 / hr First-time, walk-in One-off
Growth Creator Pack (10h bundle) 3,150 (save 1,000) Regular individual creators Pre-paid block
Recurring Monthly Podcast Retainer (8h/month) 2,500 / month Coaches, consultants, regulars Monthly subscription
Premium Launch Programme (6 sessions + strategy) 6,500 total Entrepreneurs launching a new podcast Programme fee
Corporate Corporate Podcast Programme (16h/month) 6,000–8,000 / month Businesses, PR agencies Monthly retainer
VIP Agency Partner Rate Custom Production agencies, media houses Volume contract
Revenue Scenario: If you convert just 5 clients onto the Monthly Podcast Retainer (2,500 AED/month) and land 2 Corporate Podcast Programmes (7,000 AED/month each), that alone generates 26,500 AED in guaranteed monthly recurring revenue — before a single walk-in booking.

Pricing Psychology

At 350–450 AED/hour, Dubai Podcast Studio is priced as a mid-tier studio. Given the equipment quality (Netflix-approved cameras, Shure SM7B, Sony A7S III), the location (Business Bay), and the client calibre (Yomi Denzel, Oussama Amar), this is a significant underpricing relative to the brand perception you are capable of commanding.

  • The base hourly rate should remain accessible for first-time clients — it is the entry point
  • Value should be extracted through packages, retainers, and add-ons — not by raising the hourly rate aggressively
  • Post-production editing is currently an add-on. Bundle it into higher tiers at a perceived discount to increase average order value
Section 06

Digital Marketing Strategy

Channel Priority Map

SEO
Priority 1 — Highest ROI, compounding. "Podcast studio Dubai," "record podcast Dubai," "podcast studio business bay," "video podcast studio Dubai" — these are high-intent, zero-competition-cost searches. Every person searching these terms is already a qualified buyer. This is the single best investment in the digital stack.
Google Ads
Priority 1 — Immediate traffic. Run search ads against the same high-intent keywords while SEO builds. Budget: 3,000–5,000 AED/month. Target: search terms with "book," "rent," "record," "hire." These convert at the highest rate of any paid channel for a service business.
Instagram
Priority 2 — Brand & social proof. The visual nature of the studio (lighting, cameras, premium equipment) is perfectly suited to Instagram. Reels showing the studio in action, client testimonials, and behind-the-scenes content drive both aspiration and direct bookings.
TikTok
Priority 2 — Discovery & virality. Short-form video of the studio, "what it's like to record here," creator guest appearances, and "studio tour" content regularly goes viral in the creator economy niche. One viral video can generate hundreds of inquiries.
LinkedIn
Priority 2 — B2B pipeline. This is the primary channel for reaching corporate clients, PR agencies, and business decision-makers in Dubai. Content: thought leadership about podcast as a brand-building tool, case studies of corporate clients, direct outreach to CMOs and Communications Directors.
Email / CRM
Priority 3 — Retention & upsell. With 3,500+ past clients, you have a significant database. A monthly email to past clients (studio news, new packages, exclusive offers) costs almost nothing and will generate consistent re-bookings. This is currently a completely untapped revenue channel.
YouTube
Priority 3 — Long-term authority. A YouTube channel documenting the studio, interviewing notable clients, and producing educational content about podcasting in Dubai builds SEO, brand authority, and organic discovery over time.

SEO — The Non-Negotiable Foundation

The website currently has multiple pages targeting podcast studio keywords but lacks a coherent SEO strategy. Immediate actions:

  • Audit and optimise title tags, meta descriptions, and H1s for all pages using target keywords
  • Create a dedicated blog/resource section: "How to start a podcast in Dubai," "Best microphones for podcasting," "Podcast equipment guide UAE" — these attract organic traffic from creators researching the space
  • Build citations on Google Business Profile, Tripadvisor, Dubai tourism directories, and relevant local directories
  • Generate authentic Google reviews — with 3,500+ past clients, a single email campaign requesting reviews should produce 100+ reviews rapidly

Paid Media — Budget Recommendation

Recommended Monthly Ad Budget

Total: 8,000–12,000 AED/month — Google Search: 4,000–5,000 AED · Instagram/Meta: 2,500–4,000 AED · LinkedIn (B2B campaigns): 1,500–3,000 AED

Start with Google Search only in month one — it delivers the highest intent traffic with the lowest creative overhead. Add Meta and LinkedIn in month two once conversion tracking is verified and the testimonials/social proof on the website has been fixed.

Section 07

Content Strategy

A podcast studio that does not produce compelling content about itself is the equivalent of a restaurant with no photos on its menu. The studio's visual and acoustic environment is the product — content is how you sell it without selling it.

Content Pillars

Pillar 01

The Studio Experience

Show what it feels like to record here. Studio tours, equipment walkthroughs, lighting setups, "day in the life" content. Let the product sell itself visually.

Pillar 02

Client Spotlights

Feature clients (with permission) — their podcast, their story, what they recorded. This is social proof that also provides value to the featured client. Mutual amplification.

Pillar 03

Podcasting Education

"How to launch a podcast in Dubai," "What microphone should you use," "Solo vs. interview format." Educational content attracts SEO traffic and positions the studio as the authority.

Pillar 04

Dubai Creator Scene

Coverage of the broader Dubai creator ecosystem — events, trends, notable voices. Positions the studio as the hub of the scene, not just a service provider.

Content Calendar — Monthly Cadence

  • Instagram Reels (3/week): Studio footage, client clips, equipment features, "before/after" audio quality comparisons
  • TikTok (3–5/week): Studio tours, "what creators say after recording here," trending audio formats, Dubai creator content
  • LinkedIn (2/week): Thought leadership on corporate podcasting, B2B case studies, industry stats about podcast growth in UAE
  • Blog / SEO (2/month): Long-form articles targeting high-value search terms
  • Email Newsletter (1/month): Past client re-engagement, new packages, featured clients, studio news
  • YouTube (2/month): Studio tour, client feature video, educational content
High-Leverage Content Idea: Produce a 90-second "What Yomi Denzel recorded at Dubai Podcast Studio" clip (with permission). Post it natively across all platforms. This single piece of content — associating your studio with one of the most followed French-speaking entrepreneurs in the world — could drive more qualified inbound leads than months of regular content.

The Repurposing System

Every piece of long-form content (a client session, a YouTube video, a studio tour) should be cut into micro-content for Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn clips, and thumbnail assets. One shoot = 10+ pieces of content. This is not optional at this stage — it is how you compete with a lean team and limited budget.

Section 08

Partnership & B2B Strategy

Tier 1 — Immediate Revenue Partnerships

Dubai Luxury Hotels High Value

Hotels frequented by international business travellers and content creators (Four Seasons DIFC, Address Downtown, Bulgari, One&Only) regularly need to refer guests to specialist services. A formal partnership means your studio appears in their concierge guide, in-room directory, and is proactively recommended by concierge staff to creator and business guests.

  • Prepare a 1-page partnership brief with commission structure (10–15% referral fee per booking)
  • Provide co-branded collateral for the hotel to display (QR code card, in-room guide insert)
  • Target: Guest Relations Managers and Head Concierges at 5 priority hotels
PR & Communications Agencies High Volume

PR agencies in Dubai (Hill+Knowlton, Weber Shandwick, MSL, boutique local firms) are increasingly producing podcast-format content for clients. They need a reliable, professional facility and they bring multiple client relationships.

  • Offer an agency partner rate with a formal agreement (10–20% volume discount on bookings above 20 hours/month)
  • Assign a dedicated account contact for each agency partner
  • Approach via LinkedIn outreach + direct email to Head of Content or Senior Account Directors
Creator Ambassadors — Yomi Denzel & Oussama Amar Strategic

These two individuals have recorded at the studio. They have combined audiences of several million followers, predominantly in the exact demographic that visits Dubai and has purchasing power for premium services. A formal ambassador arrangement — even a simple revenue-share on referral bookings — would generate consistent high-value inbound leads at near-zero cost.

  • Reach out directly to propose a simple affiliate/ambassador structure (10% commission on bookings they refer)
  • Ask for a single social post or Story each mentioning the studio — this alone can drive 500+ profile visits
  • Create a dedicated "As recorded by Yomi Denzel" badge or mention on the website with their permission
Dubai Business Events & Conferences Medium Term

GITEX, Arab Media Forum, STEP Conference, and dozens of other annual Dubai events bring thousands of entrepreneurs and media professionals to the city. Being positioned as the official podcast partner or recommended studio for these events puts the brand in front of exactly the right audience at the right moment.

  • Approach event organisers with a sponsorship/partnership proposal 3–4 months ahead of each event
  • Offer a "Conference Special" package to attendees (discounted first session)
  • Set up a portable demo or presence at relevant expos

B2B Corporate Sales Process

  1. Build a dedicated B2B one-pager: "Dubai Podcast Studio Corporate Programmes" — case studies, packages, pricing, contact
  2. LinkedIn prospecting: 20 personalised outreach messages per week to CMOs, Communications Directors, and Heads of Marketing in Business Bay companies
  3. Offer a free 30-minute "studio experience" session for corporate decision-makers — once they see the studio, conversion is high
  4. Follow up with a custom proposal and ROI framework ("Your CEO's podcast episode reaching 10,000 listeners = equivalent PR value of X")

90-Day Execution Roadmap

Three phases. Each builds on the last. Nothing here is optional — these are the minimum viable moves to build a marketing engine that runs without constant intervention.

  • Rewrite homepage hero to lead with "3,500+ professionals from 75 countries" and Yomi Denzel / Oussama Amar client references

Week 2

  • Launch Google Search Ads targeting "podcast studio Dubai," "record podcast Dubai," "video podcast studio business bay" — initial budget 150 AED/day
  • Install Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel on all web pages — conversion tracking is non-negotiable before any paid spend

Week 3

  • Design and publish the Monthly Podcast Retainer package (2,500 AED/month) on the website
  • Begin outreach to 3 luxury hotels with the concierge partnership brief
  • Shoot a full studio tour video (30–90 seconds) for Instagram Reels and TikTok — high production quality, the studio equipment should be visible
  • Post 3x/week on Instagram and TikTok — studio footage, equipment walkthroughs, behind-the-scenes

Week 4

  • Reach out to Yomi Denzel and Oussama Amar teams to formalise ambassador/affiliate arrangement
  • Build B2B one-pager and begin LinkedIn outreach to 10 target corporate prospects
  • Review Google Ads performance — pause underperforming keywords, scale winners
  • Set up CRM (even a simple one — HubSpot free tier) to track corporate prospects
Phase 2 — Build the Machine Days 31–60 Revenue Systems

Revenue Packages

  • Launch the Podcast Launch Programme (6-session package, 6,500 AED) with a dedicated landing page
  • Launch the Corporate Podcast Programme with a case study from any existing business client
  • Add agency partner pricing page — private URL to share directly with agency prospects

Paid Media Expansion

  • Launch Meta (Instagram + Facebook) ads — Reels format, targeting Dubai-based creators, entrepreneurs, coaches aged 28–50
  • Launch LinkedIn sponsored content targeting Dubai CMOs, Communications Managers, and Business Owners
  • Implement retargeting campaigns — re-target website visitors who did not book
  • Total paid media budget this phase: 8,000–10,000 AED/month

Partnerships

  • Close minimum 2 hotel concierge partnerships
  • Close minimum 1 PR agency partner agreement
  • Host first "Podcasters Night" event at the studio — invite past clients and prospects, 20–30 attendees

Content Engine

  • Establish consistent posting rhythm: 3x/week Instagram, 3x/week TikTok, 2x/week LinkedIn
  • Launch YouTube channel with studio tour and first client feature video
  • Send first monthly email newsletter to past client database
Phase 3 — Scale What Works Days 61–90 Growth & Optimisation

Double Down

  • Identify which ad creative, channel, and audience is driving the highest-value bookings — allocate 70% of budget there
  • Scale the Monthly Retainer programme — target 5 active monthly retainer clients by end of day 90
  • Convert at least 1 corporate client onto a monthly programme

Referral Programme

  • Launch a formal referral programme: existing clients earn 200 AED studio credit for every new paying client they refer
  • Announce via email to all 3,500+ past clients — a single email to this list should generate 15–30 qualified referrals

International Push

  • If ambassador arrangement with Yomi Denzel or Oussama Amar is live — coordinate a social post during this phase
  • Target French-speaking creator communities (Facebook groups, Telegram, Discord) with the "Record in Dubai" message
  • Translate key landing pages and booking flow into French to remove friction for this audience

Review and Plan Quarter 2

  • Full performance audit: bookings, revenue per channel, CAC, top-converting packages
  • Brief Q2 roadmap based on real data — not assumptions
