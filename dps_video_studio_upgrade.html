The Most Important Camera Setting — Always HDMI Clean Output

Every camera you purchase must have clean HDMI output — meaning the HDMI port sends pure video signal with no on-screen menus, battery indicators, or overlays. Not all cameras have this. The Sony models specified above all do. Before purchasing any other camera brand, specifically verify "clean HDMI output" in the spec sheet. Without it, you cannot record the video feed to an external recorder or capture card.

Fill Light + Backlight 2 more lights per studio

Fill Light
Godox SL60W (second unit)
Same model, opposite side, 50% power
The fill light is the same model as the key — placed on the opposite side at half power. This fills shadows on the face without eliminating them. Rule: fill light always 50% brightness of key light. Use same softbox for even diffusion. Total for key + fill: AED 900.
AED 450 (second unit)
Same sources as key light
Without a fill light, one side of every face is in shadow. This is the most common mistake in podcast video setups — fixed with one extra light.
Backlight / Hair Light
Neewer RGB LED Panel
Neewer 660 PRO RGB — compact panel
Small, bright LED panel positioned behind and above the subject. Creates a subtle rim of light that separates the subject from the background — the professional "glow" visible in all high-end studio productions. Also functions as background accent lighting. RGB: match DPS brand colours.
AED 220
Amazon.ae · Sharaf DG
Without a backlight, subjects "merge" into the background visually. This AED 220 light is the difference between flat and three-dimensional.

Colour Temperature

5600K
All lights at 5600K (daylight). Never mix warm and cool in the same shot. Set camera white balance to match: 5600K manual.

Key Light Power

100%
Key light at full power. Fill light at 50%. Backlight at 30–40%. This ratio creates natural, three-dimensional shadow structure.

Key Light Angle

45° / 45°
Rembrandt positioning: 45° to the side, 45° above eye level. Universally flattering, standard in professional portrait and interview production.

07 — Camera Settings

Exact Camera Settings — Sony FX30

Save these as a custom preset on the camera. Every session starts with these settings locked. No engineer should be adjusting settings mid-session.

Resolution

4K / 25fps
4K for future-proofing. 25fps for UAE market (PAL standard). Never use 60fps for podcast — too smooth, removes cinematic feel.

Shutter Speed

1/50s
Always 2× the frame rate. At 25fps: 1/50s shutter. This is the 180-degree rule — produces natural motion blur on any movement.

ISO

800–1600
Studio lighting means low ISO is achievable. Keep below 3200 to avoid noise. With Aputure Amaran at full power: ISO 800 is achievable.

White Balance

5600K Manual
Never use Auto White Balance — it drifts during a session. Set manually to match your lights: 5600K if using daylight LEDs.

Picture Profile

S-Cinetone
Sony's built-in cinematic colour profile. Warm, flattering skin tones, natural contrast. No colour grading knowledge needed — looks professional straight out of camera.

Autofocus

Real-Time Eye AF
Sony's eye-tracking AF locks onto the host's eye and never loses focus. The engineer never needs to touch the focus during a 90-minute session. Set to Human → Eye AF.
