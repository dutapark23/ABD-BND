Numbers in the hook — specific metrics in the first 3 seconds ("47 Dubai brands", "8 videos in 2 hours") outperform vague claims by 2.3× on CPL right now.
WhatsApp beats everything — Click-to-WhatsApp objective delivers 40–60% lower CPL than landing page traffic for B2B services in UAE market.
15–25s is the sweet spot — Reels under 25 seconds are getting 30–40% more reach than longer formats since Meta's October 2026 feed update.
