Ad A — FOMO Reel · Cold + Warm · "Your Competitor"
"Your competitor
booked a session
last week."
The most powerful trigger in a competitive market like Dubai: someone else is moving. No need to name competitors, attack anyone, or oversell. One line creates the itch. Works cold and warm. Forces a decision — book now or watch your market go to someone who already did.

Hook — Lean in, conversational, slightly conspiratorial
Low energy at the start. Like you're telling them something they don't know. Slight pause after "last week."
"Your competitor was in our studio last week. They recorded a full video episode, 7 clips, and an audiogram. In two hours."
0:03–0:11
The Twist — Reframe the real cost of not acting
Calm. No aggression. This is a conversation with someone who respects their own time.
"By the time you're done watching this, they've already distributed that content to their entire network. Their algorithm is warming up. Their clients are watching. Yours aren't seeing anything yet."
0:11–0:18
CTA — One action, no options
Calm urgency. This isn't panic — it's a simple practical decision.
"We have slots open this week. Message us on WhatsApp now and you'll be live before November is over."

Execution Notes
Don't name the competitor — "your competitor" is more powerful than any real name. The viewer fills in whoever they're actually worried about. Universal and personal at the same time.
Tone is key — this is not a threat or a taunt. It's a friendly heads-up from someone who knows something you don't. Calm, not loud.
B-roll option — if you want a visual cut at 0:03, show the studio door closing or an empty chair in the booth. Suggests a session just ended. Seconds of footage only, then back to face cam.
Rotate the vertical — "Your competitor in real estate / in consulting / in finance was in our studio last week." Three separate versions of the same script. Same film, different first line. Triples your targeting precision with one shoot day.
Ad B — Objection Killer Reel · Warm · "I Don't Know What to Say"
"I don't know
what to talk
about on camera."
The single most common reason people who want to book DPS don't book DPS. This ad finds every warm lead who's been circling the idea for weeks, names their exact fear, and removes it in 20 seconds. Highest conversion potential of any warm-audience video right now.

Hook — Say their exact thought out loud
Say this like you've heard it a hundred times. No judgment — just recognition. This line alone is why the viewer stops scrolling: someone finally said it.
"'I don't know what to talk about on camera.' That's the number one thing we hear before every single session."
0:03–0:14
The Reframe — Kill the objection with specificity
Shift to warm confidence. You've solved this before. Many times.
"Here's what we do: before your session, we spend 15 minutes on the phone. We ask you 5 questions about your work. By the time you sit in front of the camera, you already know exactly what you're saying — because you just said it to us."
0:14–0:19
Proof + Result
Quick, confident. One breath.
"Every single client who walked in thinking they had nothing to say left with a full episode and 7 clips they were proud of."
0:19–0:22
CTA
Gentle. This person is already 80% convinced — don't oversell.
"Message us on WhatsApp. The prep call is free. Come in ready."
On-Screen Text Overlays

0:03 — "We prep you. 15 min. Before your session."
0:14 — "Full episode + 7 clips · Every time"
0:19 — End card: "Dubai Podcast Studio · WhatsApp →"
Execution Notes
The hook is the ad — say it in quotes, slightly slower, like you're quoting a real person. The viewer who has thought this exact thing will feel seen in the first 2 seconds. That's the conversion mechanism.
The "prep call" must exist — only use this script if DPS genuinely does a brief pre-session prep conversation. If the format is different (an intake form, a questionnaire), swap the detail to match your actual process.
Warm audience only — don't run this cold. The objection-killer format assumes the viewer already knows what DPS is. Cold audiences need the offer first, the objection second.
Optional variant — same script, different hook: "Everyone who came to us said 'I'm not interesting enough for a podcast.'" Swap and A/B test after week 2.
