Filming Notes
No intro, no name — go straight to "If you're going to GITEX next week." The self-filter hook ("if you're going to GITEX") immediately stops the right person and lets the wrong person scroll. That's exactly what you want.
One take feel — aim for take 3–5, not take 15. Natural > polished. This is the format that clocks 28% lower CPA for premium B2B services.
Audio quality — record using the studio mic setup. The audio quality itself is a sales argument for a podcast studio — it has to be immaculate.
Targeting — Broad · UAE · Business owners, founders, directors · Age 30–55. Let Andromeda find the audience via the creative. Do NOT layer GITEX interest targeting — that audience is too small and Meta will under-deliver.
Budget & timeline — AED 100/day · Oct 1–12 (12 days · AED 1,200 total). Objective: Leads → Click-to-WhatsApp. Pre-fill message: "I want to book a session before GITEX."
Why pre-event beats post-event — you reach people in preparation mode (high intent, low noise), before 50 other studio ads hit them post-event. CPMs are also 15–20% lower pre-event than during/post-GITEX week.
Launch Plan
Pre-GITEX Launch Plan — Oct 1 → Oct 12 (2027)
Launch (Oct 1)
Start at AED 100/day. Objective: Leads → Click-to-WhatsApp. Broad UAE, age 30–55, no interest layer. Pre-fill WhatsApp: "I want to book before GITEX." Total budget: AED 1,200.
Mid-Run Check (Oct 6)
If CPL is under AED 150, scale to AED 150/day for the final 6 days. If CPL is above AED 250, test a new hook angle — the audience is right, the hook may need adjusting.
Kill Switch (Oct 12)
Pause on Oct 12, the day GITEX opens. Urgency expires. Archive the creative — relaunch with updated dates at GITEX 2027. Do not run during the event itself.
