The Critical Distinction Nobody Says Out Loud

Developer brochures quote gross yields of 15–20%. The honest net yield — after OTA commissions (15–20%), property management (15–25%), maintenance, utilities, withholding tax, and vacancy — is typically 6–12% depending on management quality. The gap between projected and actual is where most French investors get burned. Being the channel that publishes this honestly makes you the most trusted voice in the French Bali real estate space — permanently.

The Legal Reality French Investors Don't Understand

Foreigners cannot own freehold land in Indonesia. The two main structures — PT PMA (foreign-owned Indonesian company, access to freehold) and Leasehold (typically 25–30 years + extensions) — are each misunderstood by 90% of French buyers. The PT PMA requires minimum investment of IDR 10 billion (≈ €600,000) and ongoing compliance. Leasehold is misrepresented as "quasi-ownership" when it is not. A clear French-language explainer of these structures would be the most-searched Bali real estate content in French on YouTube.
