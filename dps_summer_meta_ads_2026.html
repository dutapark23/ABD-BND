00 — Context

The Summer Brief

Summer in Dubai empties the city of tourists and sends many GCC nationals abroad. But it leaves behind a high-value residual market of entrepreneurs, corporate marketing teams, and expat professionals — people with more free time and real intent to act. Our ads speak exclusively to them.

Core Strategic Insight

Summer is not a threat — it is a selection filter. The people still in Dubai in July and August are the most committed, most motivated professionals. They did not leave because they are building something. These are our ideal clients. Every ad in this brief is written for them.

What We Are NOT Doing

No price-war messaging. No "50% off" panic discounts. No begging for bookings. Every line of copy protects the premium brand that built DPS's #1 Google Review position. Summer deals are framed as access, not desperation.

Creative Principle

Hook in 1.5 seconds (thumb-stop). Identity tap (speak to who they think they are, not what they do). One clear CTA. Each ad tested with two copy variants — headline A/B only, same visual, to isolate the winning message fast.

Why "Book a Tour" Beats "Book a Session"
Benchmark: B2B SaaS CRO Principle Applied to Physical Space

In B2B sales, the biggest conversion bottleneck is commitment size. Asking a marketing manager to approve a corporate session booking requires a budget sign-off, vendor approval, and scheduling alignment — weeks of friction. Asking them to spend 20 minutes on a free studio tour? That's a no-brainer yes. Close rate from qualified tour to booking is typically 40–60% for premium creative spaces. The tour is not a cost — it is the sales mechanism.

06 — Budget

Budget Allocation

Recommended spend for a 10-week summer campaign (June 1 → Sep 15). Adjust to your actual budget — the percentage split stays the same. Minimum viable budget to get meaningful data: AED 3,000/month.

Budget Principle

Start all 4 ads simultaneously at equal spend for the first 7 days. At day 7, pause the lowest-performing ad and double the budget of the top performer. Repeat at day 14. By week 3, you are running the winning ad with 60–70% of budget. This is standard Meta media buying practice.

The One Metric That Overrides All Others

Cost per booking (CPB) — not CPL. A lead is worthless until it books. Set up a Meta pixel conversion event on the booking confirmation page, then optimise every ad for that event by week 3. Everything else is a diagnostic. CPB is the verdict.

08 — Production

Creative Production Notes

You have 6 staff. Production must be lean. Here is exactly how to produce all 4 ads in a single half-day shoot.

What You Need to Shoot

  • Empty Dubai highway / street at 6–7am (Ad 01 hook) — iPhone on a tripod, slow-motion, 15 sec
  • Someone entering the DPS studio, adjusting mic, putting on headphones — studio lights on (Ad 01, 03)
  • Studio overview shots — all 7 studios, equipment close-ups (Ad 04)
  • Person on camera talking (Ad 03 testimonial) — raw, 90 seconds of talking points
  • Phone screen recording showing "podcast stats" (you can mock this in Spotify)

What You Do Not Need to Shoot

  • No fancy camera gear — iPhone 15 Pro is better than a DSLR for Instagram Reels in 2025
  • No professional lighting crew — DPS studios already have broadcast lighting
  • No voice-over artist — music + text overlays carry Ads 01 and 02 entirely
  • No external location hire — every shot is either inside DPS or on a public Dubai street
  • No post-production agency — CapCut or Premiere Rush handles the edits

Editing Instructions (CapCut)

  • All Reels: 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920px minimum
  • Static/Carousel: 1:1 square, 1080×1080px — clean dark background
  • Subtitles on all Reels (85% of Instagram watched without sound)
  • Font: Bold Sans-Serif (DPS gold for key words, white for body text)
  • No transitions — hard cuts only. Transitions signal amateur editing in 2025/26

Music Licensing (Royalty-Free)

  • Epidemic Sound (subscription ~$15/mo) — best for Dubai business/premium feel
  • Search tags: "cinematic dark minimal", "building tension instrumental"
  • Do NOT use trending IG audio for conversion ads — it distracts from the message
  • Keep music at -18db so it sits under text/visuals, not over them
  • Fade out in last 3 seconds of Reels — creates a clean moment for the CTA
